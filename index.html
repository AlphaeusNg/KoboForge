Auto · waiting for PDF

Auto keeps ordinary documents reflowable and switches graphic-heavy PDFs to a fixed-layout EPUB 3 facsimile. Fixed layout preserves exact positions, fonts, sidebars, image wraps, and writing space, but its text cannot be resized or edited like a normal ebook.

Workflow
  1. Import a document (DOCX, PDF, TXT, or Markdown).
  2. Keep Auto, or choose exact fixed pages versus editable reflowable text.
  3. Choose your Kobo; detected DOCX/PDF images are optimized for it automatically.
  4. For Reflowable, edit directly in the selected Kobo view and use Diff before export.
  5. For Fixed, page through the locked source-faithful Kobo preview, then export.
